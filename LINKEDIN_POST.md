🎉 **Every celebration deserves to be thoughtfully planned — not lost in messy WhatsApp chats.**

Excited to unveil **Gatherly AI**, an AI-powered celebration and event planning workspace built for **PromptWars Virtual — Challenge 5: Celebration & Event Planner**! 🇮🇳✨

### 💡 The Problem
Whether planning a 250-guest Jaipur wedding, a 60-guest rooftop milestone birthday in Bhopal, or an 800-student campus cultural fest in Bengaluru, planners face the same nightmare:
- AI chatbots spit out walls of generic text that you can't edit, save, or track.
- Budget calculations drift due to floating-point errors.
- Unscheduled deadlines lead to last-minute vendor scrambles.
- Unreliable internet at banquet halls makes cloud-only tools fail.

### 🚀 The Solution: Gatherly AI
Instead of an ephemeral chatbot, Gatherly AI translates your brief into a live, interactive, editable celebration workspace:
1. **Deterministic INR Budget Engine**: Zero mathematical drift. Live integer rupee arithmetic for quotes, advance deposits, and category distributions.
2. **Relative Timeline & Checklist**: Translates AI day-offsets into verified calendar deadlines with `date-fns`, tracking overdue tasks and milestone dependencies.
3. **Vendor Negotiation Pipeline**: Status tracking (from inquiry to booked) with crucial cultural questions to ask caterers and venues.
4. **Transparent Event Readiness Score (0-100)**: Multi-factor mathematical formula evaluating checklist progress, vendor readiness, budget discipline, and weather risks.
5. **100% Offline-First Privacy**: Stored locally in `localStorage` with React 19's `useSyncExternalStore`, complete with JSON backup import/export and a print-ready PDF view.
6. **Robust AI Integration**: Server-side Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite SDK (`@google/genai`) with structured JSON schema outputs and resilient category normalization—falling back to an instant offline generator when running without an API key.
